Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need any coding or hardware experience?

No prior experience is necessary. We have workshops tailored for complete beginners where you will write your first lines of code and build physical circuits.

Q: How much does it cost?

0 dollars. Hack Club Marina operates under the 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsorship of Hack Club Bank (The Hack Foundation, EIN: 81-2908499). All equipment, kits, and resources are provided for free.

Q: What happens if I miss a meeting?

No problem! All workshop materials, notes, and hackathon updates are posted in our group chat so you can catch up anytime.
