Hack Club Marina Constitution

Official bylaws governing the Hack Club Marina Chapter of Marina High School, establishing membership rights, officer responsibilities, 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsorship under Hack Club Bank (The Hack Foundation, EIN: 81-2908499), and club meeting procedures.

Preamble Art. I Art. II Art. III Art. IV Art. V Art. VI Art. VII Art. VIII Art. IX Art. X

Preamble

We, the students of Marina High School, in order to foster passion for technology, hands-on computer science, maker culture, and collaborative engineering, do hereby establish this constitution for the Hack Club Marina Chapter.

Article I - Name

The name of this organization shall be Hack Club Marina Chapter.

Article II - Purpose

The purpose of this organization shall be to create an inclusive, student-led nonprofit space where students of all skill levels learn to code, collaborate on hands-on software and hardware projects, and access global technical resources, hackathons, and grants under the 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsorship of Hack Club Bank and the support of the main Hack Club organization.

Article III - Membership

Section I

The membership of Hack Club Marina Chapter shall consist of any student enrolled at Marina High School.

Section II

Active members shall attend meetings regularly and participate in club build sessions and projects.

Article IV - Officers

The officers of Hack Club Marina Chapter shall consist of President, Vice President, Secretary, and Treasurer/Technical Lead.

Article V - Duties of Officers

Section I · President

The President shall oversee club operations, lead meetings, and serve as the primary contact with school administration and the national Hack Club organization.

Section II · Vice President

The Vice President shall assist in planning workshops, attend meetings regularly, and fulfill the President's duties in their absence.

Section III · Secretary

The Secretary shall record meeting notes, manage member communications, and track project submissions.

Section IV · Treasurer / Technical Lead

The Treasurer/Technical Lead shall manage club accounts, oversee fundraising or hardware requests, and support workshop activities. There shall be multiple roles in specific departments to manage hardware, software, communications, and fundraising.

Article VI - Elections

Section I

Eligibility for office requires a minimum 2.0 GPA, good standing with school administration, and active participation in club activities.

Section II

Elections will not be conducted; it is on a volunteer basis.

Article VII - Finances, Fees, Dues

Section I · No Dues

Membership dues will NOT be collected. Hack Club Marina Chapter is 100% free for all students.

Section II · Asset Reversion

If Hack Club Marina Chapter becomes inactive or disbands, all assets of the club will revert to the Associated Student Body general account for the Interclub Council.

Section III · Fiscal Sponsorship

Funds to operate Hack Club Marina Chapter shall be raised through approved fundraising activities, ASB allocations, hardware/software grants, and fiscal sponsorship donations. Hack Club Marina operates under the 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsorship of Hack Club Bank (The Hack Foundation, EIN: 81-2908499), ensuring all charitable contributions and grants received are tax-deductible to the fullest extent permitted by law.

Article VIII - Activities and Projects

Hack Club Marina Chapter shall host coding workshops, collaborative build sessions, and project showcases throughout the school year.

Article IX - Amendments

This constitution may be amended by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of eligible active members of Hack Club Marina Chapter.

Article X - Meetings

Section I · Regular Meetings

Hack Club Marina Chapter will hold regular club meetings on a flexible schedule as announced by officers.

Section II · Location & Time

Meetings will be held during lunch or after school in Room 252 and Lunch on Mondays unless revised.

Ratified by Marina High School Student Body & Chapter Officers
