Hackathons & NASA Space Apps

At Hack Club Marina, we build real-world software, games, satellites, and hardware hacks. We participate as Marina High School squads in global hackathons, winning tech grants, cash awards, internships, and national recognition.

Kickoff Event: November 14–15th
Official NASA Global Hackathon

NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026

The NASA International Space Apps Challenge is the world's largest annual global hackathon. For 48 continuous hours, coders, scientists, designers, storytellers, makers, and technologists across the globe come together to solve challenges faced on Earth and in space using NASA's open-access data.

Real NASA Satellites

Direct access to Landsat, MODIS, Sentinel, and NOAA satellite telemetry for climate and agriculture solutions.

Deep Space & Mars

Explore James Webb Space Telescope data, Mars Perseverance rover telemetry, and exoplanet archives.

All Skill Levels Welcome

Beginners, graphic designers, UI/UX creators, and advanced programmers all have dedicated roles in every team.

Global Awards & Judging

Teams compete for Local People's Choice awards and Global Nominee status evaluated by NASA engineers.

Event Quick Facts

  • Dates: November 14–15th, 2026 (48 Hours)
  • Cost: $0 (100% Free)
  • Location: Room 252 (Sessions) & Discord / Online Collaboration
  • Team Size: 1 to 6 students per project team
  • Deliverables: 7-slide deck or 240-second video demo + code repository

First-Time Hackathon Tips

  • You don't need to be an expert coder; project ideas, graphic wireframes, and storytelling are equally valued by judges.
  • Hack Club Marina officers will pair you with mentors and teammates who match your skill interests.
  • Free hardware kits, microcontrollers, and snacks are provided during our build sessions.

Devpost is the world's leading hackathon platform. Hack Club Marina members form teams during our Monday lunch sessions to sprint on projects for upcoming high school hackathons. Every project you submit becomes a permanent public portfolio link you can put on college and internship applications.

1. Idea & Teaming

Brainstorm with Marina HS peers during Monday Lunch in Room 252.

2. Build Session

Use free Hack Club hardware, APIs, and student mentor guidance.

3. Publish & Win

Record a 2-minute demo video and submit to Devpost judges.
